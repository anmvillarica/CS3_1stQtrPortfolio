### 1. Give 2-4 key points from the SONA 2025 that you found **interesting**
* More **Opportunities** for Filipinos **to Earn Money** for their Families
* Improvements In Terms of the **Providing of Electricity and Water**
* More **Benefits to Teachers**

### 2. Give 1-3 key points from SONA 2025 that you found **weird** for any reason
* Aiming to Plant **100 Million** Coconut Trees
* Creation of **Presidential Merit** Scholarships 
* “A **friend** to all, *an enemy to none.*”

### 3. **Reflection**: 
* > Right now, the Philippines is a place that needs to go through a lot of improvements. Consequently, the government and president have been providing numerous great ideas to help support
and improve the Filipino people's livelihoods. The first step to improvement is **to be attentive to the country's issues**, and **to be realistic but creative** with proposed solutions. SONA 2025 has shown me that the Philippines, with the amount of ideas and proposals stated by the president, *may* be at the **turningpoint into a better future.** 
* > SONA 2025 has shown me that the Philippines may not be the most advanced, disciplined, effective nation, but not all the issues are left unheard. Despite everything, we must be optimistic and supportive so that we too can help the country be better. There may be a lot of problems, but the glass should not be seen as *half-empty*. Instead, we should be thankful of the glass *half-full* of water that has **space to grow and improve**. 

### 4. What **1 word/ phrase/ or sentence** do you have for the Philippines after hearing SONA 2025?
* > Good **ideas** *promise* a great future, **but** they do not bring positive *change* until they are put into **action**.
